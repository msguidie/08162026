-- algorithm: No RL. Three families of SFP agents, all driven by a copyable forward model under a 1000-forward-model-call budget (game/budget/ActionsBudget.java, default `new ActionsBudget(1000)` in Engine.java and RoundRobinTournament.java).  1) BMRH = Branching-Mutation Rolling Horizon (players/ai/explicit/HyperRHEA.java). A (1+1) random-mutation hill climber, NOT population-based. The genome is a sequence of *actual Action objects*, each sampled from the state reached by the plan prefix (a_i = rnd(s_t{a_0..a_{i-1}})), not integer indices - this is the key trick for a state-dependent action space. Mutation picks a cut point and RE-ROLLS the whole suffix while replaying the plan.
